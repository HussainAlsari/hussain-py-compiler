Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This policy describes the information involved when you visit Hussain Compiler and use its browser-based editor. The site does not ask you to create an account.

Information you enter in the editor

Your program text, imported files, editor preferences, and course progress are handled in your browser and stored locally where needed. The editor does not send your program text to a Hussain Compiler application server. This browser storage is specific to the device and browser you use; clearing site data can remove it.

If you choose to install a Python package, the package name is used to request that package from the configured package service. Pyodide and editor resources are downloaded from their public CDNs when needed. Those providers may receive ordinary connection information, such as your IP address and browser request details, under their own privacy policies.

Hosting and technical information

The site is hosted as static pages. The hosting provider and network services may process technical request data to deliver, secure, and maintain the site. Hussain Compiler does not operate a user account database through this editor.

Advertising

The current source includes an AdSense publisher account identifier, but does not load the Google advertising script or display ads. If advertising is enabled in the future, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies and process data as described in Google's published policies. This policy and the site's consent setup must be updated before ads are served. For users in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, publishers serving ads must use a Google-certified consent management platform that integrates with the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework where required.

Learn more in Google's advertising and cookie information and Google's Privacy Policy.

Your choices

You can remove locally stored editor data through your browser's site-data settings. You can also block or clear cookies and storage through browser controls; some site features may then reset. Third-party providers describe their own privacy choices in their policies.

Children

This is a general-purpose programming tool and does not request age or account details. Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of the internet and third-party services.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the site or its services change. The latest revision date appears above. To ask a privacy question, use the Contact page.
